Sound familiar?

These are the problems we see most in AI-built apps. They're not your fault, and they're usually fixable.

  • It works in preview, not online.Fine on your computer or inside the AI tool, broken the moment it's deployed.
  • The deploy keeps failing.Build errors, missing packages, settings the AI never mentioned.
  • The database won't connect.The app loads, but nothing saves or nothing shows up.
  • Every fix breaks something else.You paste the error back into the AI and get a new one.
  • It works for you, not for others.Logins, links or pages that fail on other people's devices.

How we fix it
