Case study: Pharmacy skills platform

Five prototypes. One platform for 200 students.

The School of Pharmaceutical Sciences at Universiti Sains Malaysia (USM) wanted pharmacy students to practise clinical skills through guided case studies. The lecturer had already built each learning module as its own prototype. They worked, but they lived apart.

PPrototype

Separate module prototypes built by the lecturer, each with its own look and setup. Good ideas, ready to be brought together.

PProduction

  • All five modules joined into one lean system, matched with well-maintained libraries
  • One sign-in for students, with access codes
  • Hosted on a dedicated server, with backups set up
  • Ready for tablets, so students can use it beyond the lab

Case study: Research officer award

An AI-built award site, made ready for judging.

The Malaysia Research Officer Society (MROS) built a nomination site with AI for its award honouring the country's research officers. The form worked. Managing every nominee's attachments, and fairly choosing the winners, turned out to be far more work than expected.

PPrototype

A working nomination form built with AI. Attachments piled up with no easy way to sort or review them, and organisers had no view for managing entries or judging them.

PProduction

  • Rough edges in the nomination flow fixed
  • Attachments organised per nominee, easy to open and review
  • An admin view to manage every nomination
  • An audit view, so each winner decision can be traced
  • Kept on the client's own Firebase account, no migration needed

Questions

Do I need the Play Store or App Store?

No. Your app installs from the browser straight onto the home screen, on Android and iPhone. For staff-only apps on Android, we can also give you an installable APK file.

Will you change my app?

Only what it needs to run safely in public. We tell you what we changed and why. Your idea, your screens and your features stay yours.

Who owns the code?

You do. If you ever leave, we hand you everything you need to move it.

What kinds of apps can you host?

Simple HTML apps, and apps built in Node, Python or PHP with a database: booking systems, order forms, member lists, internal tools. We can host it for you, or work inside your own hosting or cloud account. Send it over and we'll tell you which plan fits.

My app has customer data. What about PDPA?

We set up the basics: a privacy notice, safe storage and backups. When we host your data, we sign simple written terms on how we handle it. For full legal advice, talk to a lawyer.

How do I know the final price?

Send us your app and we take a free look. We confirm a fixed price and timeline before any work starts. If an app needs rebuilding rather than fixing, we tell you and quote that separately.

How does payment work?

Setup is 50% deposit to start and 50% when your app is live. Cloud plans are billed at least 6 months in advance.

How fast do you reply?

Within 1 working day on Hosting and Care. Within 4 working hours on Care Plus.
